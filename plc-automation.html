PLC Basics

CPU

Executes the control program repeatedly in a scan cycle.

Digital Inputs

Read on/off devices such as pushbuttons, switches, and sensors.

Digital Outputs

Drive relays, contactors, lamps, valves, and other loads through suitable interfaces.

Analog Inputs

Read signals such as 0–10 V or 4–20 mA.

Analog Outputs

Command speed, position, pressure, or other continuously variable quantities.

HMI

Human-machine interface for status, commands, alarms, recipes, and trends.

Ladder Logic Simulator

A simple seal-in style example: START energizes a motor command; STOP drops it out.

4–20 mA Scaling Calculator
