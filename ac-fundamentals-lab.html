Beginner Start Here

  1. AC reverses direction periodically.
  2. Frequency tells how many cycles occur each second.
  3. RMS voltage is the value normally used for AC ratings.
  4. For a sine wave, peak ≈ RMS × 1.414.
  5. Power factor depends on the phase difference between voltage and current.
