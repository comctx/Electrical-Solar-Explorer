Real Power (kW)

Apparent Power (kVA)

RMS voltage times RMS current, without assuming all current contributes to useful real power.

Power Factor

Ratio of real power to apparent power. A lower power factor means more current is required for the same real power.

Power-Factor Correction Methods Technical

Fixed Capacitor

Provides a constant amount of capacitive reactive power for a relatively steady inductive load.

Automatic Capacitor Bank

Switches capacitor steps in and out as system reactive demand changes.

At the Motor

Correction may be applied near a motor so reactive current does not flow through as much upstream distribution equipment, when the design permits it.

Central Correction

A larger bank at a main bus can correct many loads together, often under automatic control.

Synchronous / Electronic VAR Support

Some rotating machines and power-electronic systems can source or absorb reactive power dynamically.

Do Not Overcorrect

Too much capacitance can create leading power factor, switching transients, resonance, or voltage-control problems.

Displacement PF vs. True PF Advanced

Displacement Power Factor

Describes the phase angle relationship between the fundamental-frequency voltage and current.

True Power Factor

Real power divided by true RMS apparent power, so it includes both phase displacement and waveform distortion.

Why the distinction matters

A nonlinear load can have nearly aligned fundamental voltage/current while still drawing distorted current and therefore have a true power factor below one.

Measurement

Power-quality analyzers can separate fundamental and harmonic quantities and report true RMS, power factor, THD, and individual harmonic components.

Harmonic Mitigation Advanced

Line Reactor / DC Choke

Adds impedance that can reduce current peaks and some harmonic current in drive or rectifier applications.

Passive Harmonic Filter

Uses tuned inductors, capacitors, and sometimes resistors to provide low-impedance paths or shape system impedance at selected frequencies.

Active Harmonic Filter

Measures distortion and injects compensating current electronically to reduce selected harmonic currents.

Multi-Pulse / Low-Harmonic Drives

Special rectifier arrangements or active-front-end drives can reduce line-side harmonic current.

Detuned Capacitor Bank

Series reactors shift the capacitor-bank resonance away from dominant harmonics and limit harmonic current in systems designed for this approach.

System Study

Large capacitor or filter projects should consider source impedance, transformer size, existing harmonics, resonance, switching, and future load changes.

Resonance & Capacitor-Bank Cautions Advanced

Parallel Resonance

System inductance and capacitance can create a high-impedance condition near a harmonic frequency, magnifying voltage distortion.

Series Resonance

A low-impedance path near resonance can draw large harmonic current through capacitors or reactors.

Switching Transients

Energizing capacitor banks can create high inrush current and transient voltage depending on system conditions and switching method.

Protection

Capacitor banks may require dedicated fuses, breakers, discharge resistors, contactors, unbalance protection, temperature monitoring, and rated switching equipment.

Power-factor correction and harmonic filters can materially change system impedance and fault/transient behavior. Real installations should be engineered from measured system data and equipment ratings rather than sized from a single power-factor number alone.
