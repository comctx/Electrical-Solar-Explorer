Beginner Start Here

  1. A sensor detects a condition such as position, metal, light, or an object.
  2. NO/NC describes the output state in the sensor's normal/not-detected condition.
  3. A typical 3-wire PNP sensor sources positive voltage/current to its output when active.
  4. A typical 3-wire NPN sensor sinks its output toward 0 V when active.
  5. PNP sensors are commonly paired with sinking PLC inputs; NPN sensors with sourcing PLC inputs. Always verify the actual hardware documentation.
