Beginner Start Here

  1. Three-phase systems use three AC waveforms separated by 120 electrical degrees.
  2. Wye: line voltage is √3 times phase voltage; line current equals phase current.
  3. Delta: line voltage equals phase voltage; line current is √3 times phase current.
  4. Balanced three-phase apparent power is √3 × line voltage × line current.
  5. Swapping any two phases reverses phase sequence and can reverse many three-phase motors.
