Clamp Meter Concepts

Clamp one conductor

For ordinary current measurement, the clamp surrounds one current-carrying conductor so its magnetic field can be measured.

Do not clamp the whole cord

If equal current flows out and back through conductors inside the same clamp, their magnetic fields largely cancel.

AC clamp meters

Traditional transformer-style clamps measure AC current. Many modern meters also measure DC using Hall-effect sensing.

Inrush mode

Some clamp meters capture short starting-current events from motors, transformers, and other loads.

ModeWhat it tells you

Insulation Resistance Testing

Megohmmeter

Applies a specified DC test voltage and measures very high resistance through insulation.

Why ordinary ohms mode differs

A normal multimeter uses a much lower test voltage and cannot stress insulation the same way.

Insulation testers can generate hundreds or thousands of volts. Equipment must be isolated, discharged, and tested according to manufacturer and safety procedures. Sensitive electronics can be damaged if they are left connected.

Logic Probe Basics

LOW

Input voltage falls within the probe's recognized low logic range.

HIGH

Input voltage falls within the recognized high logic range.

Pulse

The signal is changing too quickly to interpret as a steady high or low with a simple indicator.

Logic thresholds depend on the logic family and supply voltage. TTL, CMOS, and modern low-voltage logic families do not all use the same thresholds.

Common Measurement Mistakes

MistakeWhy it matters
Current lead left in amp jackTrying to measure voltage with the lead still in a current input can create a short circuit.
Resistance test on energized circuitCan damage the meter and produce meaningless readings.
Wrong CAT ratingMeter category and voltage ratings must be appropriate for the measurement environment and transient energy.
Unfused or improperly fused current inputCan make a current-measurement mistake much more dangerous.
Scope ground clip on the wrong pointBench oscilloscopes often have earth-referenced grounds; incorrect connection can short a circuit to earth.

Measurement Safety

Use test equipment rated for the circuit and environment. Inspect leads and probes, use the correct meter jacks and function, de-energize whenever practical, and verify absence of voltage with an appropriate procedure. For mains or high-energy systems, measurement technique, PPE, instrument category rating, and applicable safe-work procedures are critical.
