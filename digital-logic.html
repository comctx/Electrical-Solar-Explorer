Logic Gates

AND

Output is 1 only when all required inputs are 1.

OR

Output is 1 when at least one input is 1.

NOT

Inverts the input.

NAND

NOT-AND; universal gate.

NOR

NOT-OR; universal gate.

XOR

For two inputs, output is 1 when the inputs differ.

Memory & Sequencing

DeviceFunction
LatchStores one bit while controlled by enable/set-reset behavior.
Flip-flopStores a bit, commonly changing state on a clock edge.
CounterAdvances through a sequence of binary states.
RegisterStores multiple bits.
MultiplexerSelects one of several inputs.
DecoderConverts a coded input into one-of-many outputs.

ADC & DAC

ADC

Converts an analog voltage into a digital number. Resolution, reference voltage, sample rate, and noise affect accuracy.

DAC

Converts a digital code into an analog voltage or current.
