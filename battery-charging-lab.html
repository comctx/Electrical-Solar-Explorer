Beginner Start Here

  1. Series batteries add voltage.
  2. Parallel strings add amp-hour capacity.
  3. Energy is approximately volts × amp-hours = watt-hours.
  4. Higher load current causes more internal voltage drop.
  5. Charging method depends on battery chemistry and manufacturer limits.
