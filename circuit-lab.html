How to Use Circuit Lab

  1. Choose a voltage — 12 V is a good place to start.
  2. Add components — tap Switch, Resistor, Lamp, LED, Motor, Fuse, or Ammeter.
  3. Tap a component to edit it — change resistance, ratings, or open/close a switch.
  4. Watch the live readings — current, resistance, power, and voltage drops update automatically.
  5. Use the virtual multimeter — choose DC Volts, Resistance, or Continuity, then move the red and black probes.
  6. Try the Parallel Branch Lab — turn branches on and off and watch the branch currents add together.
Tip: Start with the Series Lamp Demo or Parallel Lamp Demo to see a working circuit before building your own.

Circuit Nodes

Meter habit: voltage is measured on an energized circuit when appropriate; resistance and continuity are checked only on a de-energized circuit. This simulator uses a simplified resistance model. Real equipment may require isolation, discharge, and manufacturer procedures before resistance testing.
