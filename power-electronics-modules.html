A voltage-controlled semiconductor switch often used at lower to medium voltages and high switching frequencies.

IGBT

A power semiconductor that combines MOS-gate control with a bipolar conduction structure. Common in higher-power motor drives and inverters.

High power

Common Module Topologies

Single Switch

One MOSFET or IGBT die or switch position in a module package.

Half Bridge

High-side and low-side switches form one inverter leg.

Full Bridge / H-Bridge

Four switches allow current through a load in either direction.

Six-Pack

Three half bridges—six main switches—form a complete three-phase inverter stage.

Intelligent Power Module

Power switches plus integrated gate drive, protection, or sensing functions.

Power Stage / Stack

Module, busbars, capacitors, cooling, drivers, sensors, and mechanical structure assembled as one high-power subsystem.

Silicon, SiC & GaN

Silicon IGBT / MOSFET

Mature, widely used technologies covering a broad range of power applications.

SiC MOSFET

Silicon-carbide devices can switch high voltages efficiently and at higher frequencies than many traditional silicon alternatives.

GaN

Gallium-nitride power devices are especially useful for very fast switching in suitable voltage and power ranges.

Thermal Management

Electrical losses become heat. Junction temperature depends on power loss and the total thermal resistance from semiconductor junction to case, heatsink/cold plate, and ambient/coolant.
Power modules can operate at lethal voltages and enormous fault currents. DC-link capacitors may remain charged after input power is removed. High-power inverter and drive hardware should be serviced only with the proper procedures, test equipment, discharge verification, and manufacturer documentation.
