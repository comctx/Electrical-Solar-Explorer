Capacitor Types & Uses

A quick guide to common capacitor families, polarity, and where they are usually found.

Ceramic Small, non-polarized, inexpensive, and common in high-frequency bypassing, decoupling, timing, and RF circuits. Non-polarized
Aluminum Electrolytic High capacitance for power-supply filtering and energy storage. Usually polarized and commonly used at lower frequencies. Usually polarized
Tantalum Compact polarized capacitors with stable capacitance. Often used in electronics where space matters. Polarized
Film Non-polarized, stable, and often used in filters, audio, timing, snubbers, and AC applications. Non-polarized
Supercapacitor Very high capacitance for short-term energy storage, backup power, memory hold-up, and burst-energy applications. Often polarized
Motor Start / Run Special AC capacitors used with single-phase motors. Start capacitors are intermittent-duty; run capacitors are continuous-duty. Application-specific
