Gateways & Protocol Converters Technical

Serial-to-Ethernet

Connects legacy serial devices to an Ethernet-based system while preserving or translating the application protocol as designed.

Protocol Translation

Maps data points between systems, for example between Modbus registers and another protocol's objects or tags.

Media Conversion

Converts copper Ethernet to fiber without necessarily changing the higher-level network protocol.

Data Concentrator

Collects information from many IEDs or meters and presents it upstream through fewer connections.

Time Synchronization

Accurate timestamps help sequence events and analyze faults. Systems may use NTP, PTP, IRIG-B, GNSS/GPS-derived time, or other methods.

Configuration

Addressing, baud rate, VLANs, protocol parameters, point maps, scaling, and security settings must agree across the communicating devices.

IEC 61850 & Substation Communications Advanced

Logical Nodes & Data Models

IEC 61850 organizes protection, measurement, control, and status information into standardized object models instead of only raw register numbers.

GOOSE

Fast peer-to-peer Ethernet messaging used for time-critical status and protection/control signals within suitable substation designs.

Sampled Values

Digitized current and voltage samples can be communicated over process-bus networks in compatible architectures.

Station Bus / Process Bus

Station-bus networks interconnect IEDs and station-level systems; process-bus architectures extend digital communications closer to primary equipment.

Fiber in Substations

Fiber is valuable around high electromagnetic fields and ground-potential differences because it does not conduct electrical current between endpoints.

Redundancy

Critical networks may use redundant paths, switches, power supplies, servers, or seamless-redundancy techniques to improve availability.

SCADA Reliability & Cybersecurity Advanced

Network Segmentation

Control networks are commonly separated into security zones with controlled pathways between operational technology and other networks.

Least Privilege

Users and systems should receive only the permissions required for their authorized functions.

Logging & Time

Consistent timestamps and centralized logs improve event reconstruction, troubleshooting, and security monitoring.

Change Control

Firmware, relay settings, PLC logic, gateway maps, and network configuration need controlled documentation and testing.

Fail-Safe Operation

Loss of communications should not automatically defeat local protection or essential safety functions; behavior is engineered for the application.

Physical Security

Network cabinets, substations, control rooms, ports, and field devices also require physical access controls appropriate to the facility.

SCADA and protection networks can control real electrical equipment. Configuration errors or unauthorized commands can create equipment damage or safety hazards. Use approved engineering, cybersecurity, testing, and change-control procedures.
