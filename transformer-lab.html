Beginner Start Here

  1. The primary winding receives AC energy.
  2. The secondary winding delivers AC energy to a load.
  3. Voltage ratio follows the turns ratio: V₂/V₁ = N₂/N₁.
  4. In an ideal transformer, current changes in the opposite direction.
  5. Real transformers have losses, heating, regulation, insulation limits, and VA ratings not modeled here.
