Beginner Start Here

  1. Mechanical rotation and magnetic fields produce electrical voltage.
  2. Frequency depends on rotational speed and pole count.
  3. Frequency (Hz) = RPM × poles ÷ 120.
  4. Excitation affects generated voltage in many alternators.
  5. Load causes current, heating, torque demand, and voltage-regulation effects.
