About the App

All Things Electrical is an interactive electrical and electronics learning/reference app. It combines explanations, calculators, simulations, test-equipment concepts, industrial controls, power electronics, communications, solar, and troubleshooting.

Learn

Start with fundamentals and move into components, controls, power systems, automation, and communications.

Explore

Use interactive calculators and simulations to see how values and conditions affect a system.

Review

Use the A–Z glossary and quizzes to reinforce terminology and concepts.

Track

Favorites, recent pages, quiz scores, and completion progress can be stored locally on your device.

Getting Around

  • Find a Topic on the home screen searches the app’s indexed subjects and tools.
  • Start Learning gives beginners a recommended path.
  • My Tools on the home page shows favorites and recently viewed pages.
  • Tap ☆ Add Favorite on a page to save it to My Tools.
  • Use Dark Mode from the floating control at the bottom of the screen.
  • The Glossary provides direct links to related Explorer pages.

Progress & Privacy

Favorites, recently viewed pages, quiz scores, and completion progress use browser/device local storage. These features do not require an account. Clearing website/app data can erase this locally stored information.

Disclaimer & Electrical Safety

All Things Electrical is an educational reference and training app. It is not a substitute for licensed electrical design, engineering, inspection, manufacturer instructions, local electrical codes, utility requirements, or qualified professional judgment. Electrical work can involve shock, fire, arc-flash, stored-energy, battery, mechanical, thermal, and other serious hazards. Users are responsible for following applicable laws, codes, permits, safety procedures, equipment instructions, and qualified-person requirements. Do not perform energized electrical work unless you are properly trained, authorized, and equipped to do so.

Examples, diagrams, calculators, simulators, and troubleshooting steps in this app are teaching aids. They do not establish that a particular installation is code-compliant, safe, suitable for a specific site, or approved by an inspector, utility, manufacturer, or other authority.

Help

If a calculator result looks unusual, recheck the units and values entered. For equipment-specific questions, use the equipment manufacturer’s documentation. For installation requirements, use the electrical rules and standards applicable to the location and equipment involved.
