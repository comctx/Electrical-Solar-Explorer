Protection Devices

Fuse

Melts and opens when current exceeds its time-current capability.

Circuit Breaker

Automatically opens on specified overcurrent conditions and can normally be reset.

GFCI / RCD

Detects imbalance between conductors that can indicate leakage to ground/earth.

AFCI

Detects signatures associated with certain hazardous arcing conditions.

Surge Protective Device

Limits transient overvoltage by diverting surge energy.

Thermal Overload

Protects motors from sustained overload heating; not a substitute for short-circuit protection.

Fuse vs. Breaker

FeatureFuseBreaker
ResettableNoUsually yes
SpeedAvailable from very fast to time-delayDepends on trip technology and curve
Current limitingSome fuses are strongly current-limitingSome breakers are current-limiting
MaintenanceReplace after operationReset after cause is corrected, unless damaged

Surge & Transient Parts

MOV

Voltage-dependent device commonly used to clamp transient overvoltage.

TVS Diode

Fast semiconductor transient suppressor for lower-voltage electronics.

Gas Discharge Tube

Handles large surge energy by ionizing a gas path at high voltage.

Protection Is Layered

Different protective devices solve different hazards. Overcurrent protection, ground-fault protection, arc-fault protection, surge protection, motor overload protection, and equipment disconnecting means are not interchangeable.
