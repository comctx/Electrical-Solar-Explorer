Beginner Start Here

  1. A VFD changes AC frequency to control induction-motor synchronous speed.
  2. Synchronous RPM = 120 × frequency ÷ number of poles.
  3. An induction motor normally runs slightly below synchronous speed; that difference is slip.
  4. Below base frequency, many VFDs approximately maintain a volts-per-hertz relationship.
  5. Acceleration/deceleration ramps change how quickly the commanded frequency changes.
