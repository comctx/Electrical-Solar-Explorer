Beginner Explanation

Open this when you want the calculator to explain what each electrical value means in plain language.

Voltage (V)
Electrical pressure that pushes charge through a circuit.
Current (A)
The amount of electrical charge flowing through the circuit.
Resistance (Ω)
How much the circuit opposes current flow.
Power (W)
How quickly electrical energy is being used or delivered.
With 12 V across 6 Ω, the circuit draws 2 A and uses 24 W.
