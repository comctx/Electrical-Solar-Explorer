Lighting Technologies

Incandescent

Heats a filament until it glows. Simple but inefficient compared with modern sources.

Halogen

Incandescent lamp using halogen chemistry to operate the filament hotter and more compactly.

Fluorescent

Gas discharge produces ultraviolet light that excites phosphor coating; requires a ballast.

HID

High-intensity discharge families include metal-halide and high-pressure sodium lamps.

LED

Semiconductor light source normally powered by a current-regulating driver.

OLED

Organic electroluminescent layers emit light over an area, common in displays.

Lumens, Lux & Efficacy

Lumen

Total visible light output.

Lux

Lumens per square meter on a surface.

Lumens per watt

Luminous efficacy: visible light output per electrical watt.

Color & Quality

TermMeaning
Color temperature (CCT)Warm-to-cool appearance of nominal white light, expressed in kelvin.
CRIGeneral indicator of how colors appear relative to a reference source.
DimmingMay use phase-cut, 0–10 V, PWM, DALI, or other control depending on driver/fixture.
FlickerLight output modulation caused by driver behavior or power ripple.

Ballasts & LED Drivers

Ballast

Controls current through gas-discharge lamps and may provide starting voltage.

LED driver

Converts input power into controlled current/voltage suitable for LED strings and may provide dimming and protection.
