Beginner Start Here

  1. A transistor can let a small control signal switch a larger load current.
  2. A BJT is controlled by base current; a MOSFET is controlled primarily by gate voltage.
  3. The load and transistor must both stay within their ratings in real circuits.
  4. An inductive load such as a relay coil can create a voltage spike when switched off.
  5. A flyback diode is commonly used across a DC coil to give that current a safe decay path.
