Beginner Start Here

  1. Bonding connects conductive parts together so they remain at nearly the same potential.
  2. An equipment grounding conductor provides a low-impedance fault-current path back toward the source.
  3. Neutral is a current-carrying circuit conductor in normal operation; it is not interchangeable with equipment grounding conductors downstream of the permitted bonding point.
  4. Grounding electrodes connect a system to earth for specific functions, but earth itself is not a substitute for the intended equipment fault-current path.
  5. Shielding helps control noise/EMI; shield termination depends on the system and manufacturer design.
