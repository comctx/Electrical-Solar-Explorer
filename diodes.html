Beginner Start Here

A diode is one of the simplest semiconductor components. These six ideas explain the page before you start using the calculators.

What is a diode?

A diode is a semiconductor device designed to conduct much more easily in one direction than the other.

Anode and cathode

The two terminals are the anode and cathode. On many physical diodes, a stripe marks the cathode end.

Forward voltage

A real diode normally needs some forward voltage before substantial current flows. Silicon diodes are often around 0.6–0.8 V, but the actual value depends on current and temperature.

Reverse voltage

When reverse-biased, a normal diode blocks current until its reverse-voltage limit is exceeded. Zener diodes are intentionally designed to operate in a controlled reverse-breakdown region.

Why use a diode?

Diodes are used for rectification, reverse-polarity protection, voltage reference, signal steering, clipping, flyback protection, and light emission.

LEDs are diodes too

A light-emitting diode produces light when forward current flows. It normally needs current limiting, such as a resistor or regulated driver.

In Other Words
Forward-biased: the diode can conduct.
Reverse-biased: a normal diode blocks current.
The cathode is commonly marked with a stripe on axial diodes.

Diode Types & Uses

Common diode families and where they show up.

Silicon RectifierGeneral-purpose power rectification and reverse-polarity protection.Power / rectification
Schottky DiodeLower forward drop and fast switching, useful in low-voltage supplies and digital protection.Low Vf / fast
Zener DiodeDesigned for controlled reverse breakdown, commonly used for references, clamps, and shunt regulation.Voltage control
LEDProduces light when forward biased. Color and construction affect forward voltage.Light
PhotodiodeProduces current in response to light and is used in optical sensing and communication.Light sensing
TVS DiodeDesigned to clamp short transient overvoltage events and protect sensitive electronics.Surge protection
Fast / Ultrafast DiodeReduced reverse-recovery time for switching converters and higher-frequency rectification.Switching
Signal DiodeSmall, fast diodes used for switching, detection, logic steering, and waveform shaping.Signals
Varactor / VaricapUses junction capacitance under reverse bias as a voltage-controlled capacitor in RF circuits.RF tuning
Diode voltage, current, surge, temperature, and reverse-voltage limits vary widely. For real circuits, use the exact part datasheet and include appropriate safety margin.
