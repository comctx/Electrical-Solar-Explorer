Real-World Limits

Supply rails

Output cannot exceed the op-amp's available supply range; many devices are not truly rail-to-rail.

Bandwidth

Gain and usable frequency are linked through gain-bandwidth limitations.

Slew rate

Limits how fast output voltage can change.
